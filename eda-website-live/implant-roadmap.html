What you'll be able to do by the end.

A structured roadmap from first consult to final delivery, built for clinicians placing their first single implants.

01

Select the right cases

Know which straightforward single-implant cases to take on, and which to refer, before you pick up a handpiece.

02

Plan restoratively

Read the records and CBCT, and plan the implant position from the final restoration backwards.

03

Assess the site

Evaluate bone volume, soft tissue and site suitability, and understand when conditions are, and aren't, favourable.

04

Master the surgical foundations

Flap design, osteotomy sequence and the principles of atraumatic, well-positioned placement.

05

Place a single implant

Hands-on placement of a straightforward single implant with correct three-dimensional positioning.

06

Restore the implant

From impression or scan through to delivering a predictable, well-contoured definitive crown.

07

Maintain and review

Peri-implant health, review protocols and the long-term care that keeps your cases stable.

08

Avoid the common pitfalls

Recognise, prevent and manage the early complications that catch clinicians out.

09

Follow a repeatable workflow

Leave with a clear, step-by-step protocol you can run on your own single-implant cases on Monday.
