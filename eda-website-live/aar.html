Quick answers.

This is not a lecture course. Both days combine advanced lectures with extensive hands-on training, 8:30am to 5:00pm.

Day One Diagnosis → Occlusion → Deprogramming

Comprehensive Rehabilitation Diagnosis. A structured approach to complex cases: occlusion, vertical dimension, wear patterns, muscle involvement and functional risk.

Occlusal Philosophies, Demystified. Kois, Spear, Dawson, LVI and Pankey compared. How centric relation definitions differ across schools of thought, and how that changes clinical decision making.

CR & CO, hands-on. Identifying, verifying and recording centric relation and centric occlusion, and transitioning CO=MIP.

Neuromuscular Deprogramming, hands-on. Leaf gauges, Lucia jigs, Kois deprogrammers and anterior deprogrammers. When to use each and how to interpret patient response.

Day Two Vertical Dimension → Preparation → Delivery

Vertical Dimension Management. When increasing VDO is indicated and how to do it safely. Recording, verifying and transferring planned changes with calipers and reproducible reference points.

Orthotic Therapy & Stabilisation. Designing, placing and adjusting orthotics, transitioning into CO=MIP, and using them as diagnostic and therapeutic tools.

Advanced Preparation, hands-on. Upper and lower arch preparation for rehabilitation cases, including advanced lower arch, BOPT and vertical preparation protocols.

Equilibration, Sequencing & Case Review. Selective equilibration, stable occlusal schemes, restorative sequencing, then case-based discussion and open Q&A.

AAR is designed for experienced clinicians managing, or wanting to manage, complex restorative cases. If you are comfortable with crown and bridge and want to stop referring out worn dentitions, collapsed bites and vertical dimension cases, you are ready.

Teaching is sequential and systems based, so you understand not only how to perform each step, but why it is indicated and how it integrates into the broader rehabilitation workflow.

Hands-on sessions focus heavily on functional diagnosis, occlusal control and restorative execution, in a guided, supervised environment:

1 · Identify & record CR and CO. Find centric relation, verify it, record it, and transition CO=MIP.

2 · Deprogramming techniques. Leaf gauges, Lucia jigs, Kois deprogrammers and anterior deprogrammers.

3 · Orthotics & vertical dimension. Stabilising orthotics and recording VDO changes with calipers and reproducible reference points.

4 · Preparation & equilibration. Advanced upper and lower arch preparation, then selective equilibration of different occlusal schemes.

A highly experienced faculty team supervises throughout, challenging clinical assumptions and refining technique in real time.
