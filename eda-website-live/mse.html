Dr Thao Truong
Specialist Oral Surgeon · Course lead

Dr Truong completed a Bachelor of Medical Science with First Class Honours and a Bachelor of Dentistry with Honours at the University of Sydney, then four years of specialist training in Oral Surgery at Sydney Dental Hospital. She went on to complete a Graduate Diploma of Clinical Dentistry in Surgical Dentistry and a Doctor of Clinical Dentistry in Oral Surgery, both through the University of Sydney.

Her specialist training has given her extensive experience managing a broad range of oral surgical conditions across public hospital, university and private practice. Her principal private practice is at Sydney Specialist Oral Surgery in Crows Nest, and she works closely with the prosthodontic team at Sydney Prosthodontic Group in Chatswood, providing surgical expertise for complex restorative and implant treatment.

She is a Senior Lecturer and Clinical Educator at the University of Sydney School of Dentistry, and a Specialist Visiting Dental Officer at the Nepean Centre for Oral Health. She is passionate about sharing practical surgical knowledge, refining surgical technique, and helping clinicians develop sound judgement and confidence while understanding their own limitations.
