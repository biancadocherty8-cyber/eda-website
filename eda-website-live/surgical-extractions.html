Dr. Thao Truong
Specialist Oral Surgeon · Course lead

Dr Thao Truong is a Specialist Oral Surgeon with a strong commitment to providing thoughtful, empathetic and patient-centred care. She believes excellent surgical outcomes are built not only on technical expertise but also on careful communication, attention to detail and an understanding of each patient’s individual concerns.

Dr Truong completed a Bachelor of Medical Science with First Class Honours and a Bachelor of Dentistry with Honours at the University of Sydney. Following graduation, she undertook four years of specialist training in Oral Surgery at Sydney Dental Hospital, and subsequently completed a Graduate Diploma of Clinical Dentistry in Surgical Dentistry and a Doctor of Clinical Dentistry in Oral Surgery, both through the University of Sydney.

Her specialist training has provided extensive experience managing a broad range of oral surgical conditions across public hospital, university and private practice environments. Her principal private practice is at Sydney Specialist Oral Surgery in Crows Nest. She also works closely with the prosthodontic team at Sydney Prosthodontic Group in Chatswood, providing surgical expertise for complex restorative and implant treatment, and at East Coast Oral Surgery in Pymble she works alongside Oral Surgeons and Oral Medicine specialists, reflecting her strong belief in multidisciplinary care and considered treatment planning.

Teaching remains an important part of Dr Truong’s professional life. She is a Senior Lecturer and Clinical Educator at the University of Sydney School of Dentistry, where she is actively involved in the education and clinical development of dental students and postgraduate trainees. She is passionate about sharing practical surgical knowledge, refining surgical technique and helping clinicians develop sound judgement and confidence while understanding their own limitations.

Dr Truong also holds an appointment as a Specialist Visiting Dental Officer at the Nepean Centre for Oral Health, where she continues to contribute to specialist oral surgery care within the public health system.

Frequently asked

Everything you might be wondering.

Who is this course for?
General dentists who already have basic extraction experience and want to improve their surgical confidence and technique.
What does it cover?
The complete surgical extraction workflow, from medical and radiographic assessment through flap design, bone removal, tooth sectioning, root retrieval, wound closure and complication management.

Day one builds the foundations of safe surgical extraction.

Day two progresses into third molar surgery, minimally traumatic extraction techniques, implant-site considerations and management of surgical complications.
How hands-on is it?
The programme combines focused lectures, clinical demonstrations, case-based discussion and extensive hands-on training using surgical models and animal tissue.
What will I actually be able to do afterwards?
Work a surgical extraction as a planned sequence rather than improvising it: assessment and radiographic planning, flap design, bone removal, tooth sectioning, root retrieval and closure, with the technique for third molars and the means to manage a complication if one arises.
