Frequently asked

Everything you might be wondering.

Who is the Implant Roadmap 101 for?
General dentists who want to start placing implants, or add a predictable single-implant workflow. It's a foundational (101) programme, so you don't need prior implant experience, just a solid grounding in restorative dentistry.
How hands-on is it, really?
Genuinely hands-on across all three days, working on pig jaws and surgical models. Day one covers flap design, suturing and socket preservation. Day two is the implant placement workshop: the osteotomy sequence and drilling, placing a single implant, checking primary stability, and fitting the cover screw or healing abutment, plus freehand and guided positioning exercises. Day three moves into the restorative workflow, from second-stage and tissue development through to the final case-planning workshop. Multiple educators are on the floor throughout.
What does the syllabus cover?
Three days that follow the full lifecycle of a single implant case. Day one builds the diagnostic and surgical foundations: case selection, restorative planning, reading the CBCT, and flap design and tissue handling. Day two is prosthetically driven placement: site preparation, implant selection, the osteotomy sequence, primary stability, and freehand versus guided placement. Day three takes you from healing to the final restoration: soft-tissue profile and emergence, impressions, abutment and restoration selection, occlusion, complications and long-term maintenance. It is a foundational (101) programme, so no prior implant experience is needed.
Is there CPD credit?
Yes. The course is CPD-aligned and you'll receive a certificate of attendance with CPD documentation.
How do I pay, and what's the refund policy?
